Production recommendations

  • Use a dedicated token with minimum read/write access to the required KV v2 paths and a short TTL.
  • Connection targets are restricted by the code-level ALLOWED_VAULT_HOSTS allowlist in app.py.
  • Token verification uses Vault's auth/token/lookup-self endpoint. The token policy must permit this lookup.
  • Connection settings are process-memory-only. Restarting the app removes UI-added connections, so verify connections again after restart.
  • Do not expose this unauthenticated app to a LAN or internet. Remote deployment needs authentication, TLS, authorization, audit logging, and deployment hardening first.
